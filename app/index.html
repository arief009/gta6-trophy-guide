    Roadmap to the platinum

    55 trophies — 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze · 0 online · about 200 hours · difficulty 4/10 · 1 playthrough, 5 chapters · no difficulty settings, no New Game+, no mission replay

    1. Step 1 — Play the story

      60–80 hours

      Five chapters, roughly 100 missions. Do the side content as it shows up, and scan vehicles and study animals with the binoculars as you travel — that progress already counts toward the 100% checklist. Save manually at the start and end of every chapter: there is no mission replay, and the game has 15 manual save slots. Keep cheats off — using one disables trophies and saving.

      Missable warning: meditate at Jason's house in Chapter 1 (Lucia's balcony meditation). It is the one meditation that can be missed, and with it the trophy Positive Mental Attitude.

    2. Step 2 — The 100% checklist

      about 100 hours

      After the story, open Progress, then Stats, then 100% Completion. It is more lenient than it sounds — closer to 60% true completion. You only need 70 of the 80 challenges, 200 of the 277 base-model vehicles scanned, and 100 of the 255 animals studied. The only things it wants in full are Robberies, Underwater Discoveries, Shooting Ranges, Drone Races and Bret's Quests.

    3. Step 3 — Strangers & clean-up

      about 10 hours

      Finish the 8 stranger mission strands, the character strands (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone), and whatever miscellaneous trophies are left. Everything stays open in free roam after the story, so nothing here can be missed.
